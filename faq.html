Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using the site and working with our team.

How do I search for a property?

Use the search box on the home page or the filters on the Properties page. You can filter by status, location, type, price and bedrooms, and share the result by copying the page address.

Can I save properties to look at later?

Yes. Select the heart on any listing. Favorites are stored in your browser, so they stay on this device and no account is needed.

How do I arrange a viewing?

Open a property, send an enquiry from the form and the listing agent will contact you to agree a time. You can also use the Contact page.

Are the listing prices final?

Prices are guides set by the owner. The mortgage calculator on each sale listing gives an estimate only and does not include taxes, insurance or fees.

Can I list my own property?

Yes. Use the List your property page to send us the details and photos. An agent will review the submission and get in touch.

How do I change the content of this template?

Edit assets/js/data.js for properties, agents and blog posts. The documentation explains each field.
